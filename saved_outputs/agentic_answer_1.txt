## Educational Portfolio Allocation for Medium Risk Appetite

This allocation is designed for educational purposes and is not financial advice. It aims to balance growth potential with risk mitigation for an investor with a medium risk tolerance.

Allocation Percentages:

- Banking Sector: 35%
- Diversified Sectors (FMCG, Automobile, Energy/Conglomerate): 40%
- Information Technology (IT) Sector: 15%
- Cash/Money Market Instruments: 10%

Reasoning and Risk Suitability:

Banking Sector (35%):
The banking sector presents a mixed outlook, offering potential opportunities alongside inherent risks. A significant allocation here acknowledges its foundational role in the economy. For a medium-risk investor, this portion can be further diversified within the sector. For instance, a portion could be allocated to stocks with lower volatility like ICICIBANK.NS, while another part could be allocated to stocks with higher return potential but also higher volatility, such as SBIN.NS, reflecting a balanced approach to risk and reward. The average volatility of 19.47% is moderate, fitting a medium-risk profile.

Diversified Sectors (40%):
This is the largest allocation, emphasizing the importance of diversification to mitigate concentration risk.

FMCG examples include ITC.NS and HINDUNILVR.NS. These sectors often provide stability and defensive qualities, even if past returns have been mixed. Their volatility around 18-20% is generally manageable.

Automobile exposure such as MARUTI.NS can offer growth potential, as indicated by MARUTI.NS's positive one-year return. Its volatility of 23.14% is on the higher side but can be managed within a diversified portfolio.

Energy/Conglomerate exposure such as RELIANCE.NS can offer a blend of stability and growth. Its volatility of 19.74% is moderate.

This broad diversification across different economic drivers helps to smooth out returns and reduce the impact of downturns in any single sector.

Information Technology (IT) Sector (15%):
The IT sector has a weak outlook and high volatility, with average annualized volatility of 23.38%. A smaller, strategic allocation is included for a medium-risk investor to capture potential long-term growth if the sector recovers. This allocation should be approached cautiously, possibly through diversified IT ETFs or fundamentally strong companies, rather than individual high-volatility stocks like INFY.NS or TCS.NS, to manage downside risk. The limited exposure acknowledges the current weak outlook while keeping the door open for future upside.

Cash/Money Market Instruments (10%):
This allocation provides liquidity and a safety net. It serves to reduce overall portfolio volatility and offers dry powder to take advantage of potential investment opportunities during market dips. This is a prudent measure for a medium-risk investor to manage uncertainty.

Overall Risk Assessment:

This allocation is suitable for a medium-risk investor because:
- It avoids over-concentration in any single sector, particularly the high-volatility IT sector.
- It balances potentially higher-return, higher-volatility assets such as certain banking stocks or automobiles with more stable sectors such as FMCG.
- The inclusion of cash provides a buffer against market downturns.
- The diversification across multiple sectors aims to reduce overall portfolio volatility and downside risk.
